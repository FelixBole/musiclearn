## Balance: who covers whom

At the same written dynamic, instruments are not equally loud:

- **Trumpets and trombones** are the loudest; one at forte covers almost anything.
- **Horns** are softer-edged: roughly **two horns** balance one trumpet or trombone. That's why orchestras have four horns.
- **Woodwinds** in their low registers are easily covered. Low flute especially needs a thin accompaniment.
- **Strings** gain volume from numbers: a section of 16 violins carries a melody against a lot.

<Callout type="warn" title="Brass need rests">
Brass players tire quickly, especially in the high register. A trumpet part that plays loud and high for two minutes straight is unrealistic. Write rests, alternate players, and give the long sustained pads to the strings.
</Callout>

<Composer title="Classic colour choices">
- **Lonely, pastoral solo**: oboe in its middle register.
- **Mystery, night**: low flute or clarinet chalumeau over soft strings.
- **Heroic theme**: horns in unison in their middle register (around C3–F4, sounding).
- **Fanfare**: trumpets, middle register, with trombones below.
- **Weight and doom**: trombones and tuba low, but open intervals only (next unit).
</Composer>
