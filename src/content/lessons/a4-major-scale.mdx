## The pattern

A **major scale** is seven notes following the step pattern:

**W – W – H – W – W – W – H** (W = whole step, H = half step)

On white keys from C, the half steps fall exactly at E–F and B–C, so C major uses no black keys.

Start on any other note and you need sharps or flats to keep the pattern. D major: D E **F♯** G A B **C♯** D.

## Scale degrees — the real names of notes

Within a key, every note has a **function**. This is the ear-player's secret weapon: you stop hearing “F♯” and start hearing “the 3rd of the key”.

| Degree | Name | Solfège | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tonic | Do | home, rest |
| 2 | Supertonic | Re | passing, wants to move |
| 3 | Mediant | Mi | sweet, defines major |
| 4 | Subdominant | Fa | leans down to 3 |
| 5 | Dominant | Sol | strong, stable-ish |
| 6 | Submediant | La | warm, wistful |
| 7 | Leading tone | Ti | pulls hard up to 1 |
