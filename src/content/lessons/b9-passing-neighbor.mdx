Real melodies and inner voices aren't made only of chord tones. **Non-chord tones** (NCTs) are notes outside the current chord that connect, decorate and add tension. They're how a block-chord skeleton becomes music.

## Passing tones

A **passing tone** fills the gap between two chord tones a 3rd apart, moving **by step in one direction**.

## Neighbour tones

A **neighbour tone** steps away from a chord tone and **returns to the same note** — upper or lower neighbour.

<Example notes="E5 F5 E5 D5 E5" label="Upper neighbour (F), lower neighbour (D)" />

## Accented vs unaccented

- **Unaccented** (on a weak part of the beat): smooth, decorative.
- **Accented** (on the beat): the dissonance is emphasised — more expressive, more tension.

A sustained string pad can feel static. Give the violas or second violins a few **passing and neighbour tones** between chord changes and the pad starts to breathe — a classic trick in ambient exploration music, where harmony moves slowly but the texture must never feel frozen.
