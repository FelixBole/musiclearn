## Stacking thirds

A **triad** is three notes built in 3rds: a **root**, the **3rd** above it, and the **5th** above the root. On the staff a root-position triad looks like a snowman — three lines or three spaces in a row.

<Example notes="C4,E4,G4" chord label="C – E – G" />

Spelling tip: a triad always uses **every other letter**: C-E-G, D-F-A, B-D-F, F♯-A♯-C♯. Then adjust the accidentals to get the right quality.

## The four qualities

| Quality | Thirds (bottom, top) | 5th | Example | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Major** | M3 + m3 | Perfect | C E G | bright, stable |
| **Minor** | m3 + M3 | Perfect | C E♭ G | dark, stable |
| **Diminished** | m3 + m3 | diminished | C E♭ G♭ | tense, unstable |
| **Augmented** | M3 + M3 | augmented | C E G♯ | eerie, floating |
